    AVL Tree Algorithms & Controls

    AVL Property

    An AVL tree is a self-balancing Binary Search Tree (BST) where the height difference between left and right subtrees is at most 1 for every node.

    Balance Factor (BF) = Height(Left) − Height(Right). Valid values are in {-1, 0, 1}.

    Complexity

    • Search: O(log n)
    • Insert: O(log n)
    • Delete: O(log n)

    Rotations

    • Right Rotation (LL): Left child moves up, pivot moves down right.
    • Left Rotation (RR): Right child moves up, pivot moves down left.
    • Left-Right (LR): Left child rotates left, then pivot rotates right.
    • Right-Left (RL): Right child rotates right, then pivot rotates left.

    Tips & Keyboard Shortcuts

    • Batch Insert/Delete: Enter comma-separated numbers (e.g., 30, 10, 50, 20).
    • Mouse / Touch: Drag to pan, scroll wheel or pinch to zoom, click/tap a node to copy its value, hover to inspect its Balance Factor.
    • Shortcuts: Enter Insert, Space Pause/Resume, ←/→ Step Back/Forward, Ctrl+Z Undo, Esc Close Help.
